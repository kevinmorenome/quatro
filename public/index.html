    How to play Four

    Goal: score the most points by adding cards to lines connected to the grid.

    Every card has a color, shape and number. A line is 2–4 cards in a straight row or column where, for each of color, shape and number, the cards are either all the same or all different.

    Your turn: either play 1–4 cards in a single row or column (at least one must touch the grid, no gaps), or pass and optionally trade some cards for new ones. You refill to 4 cards after playing.

    Scoring: add up the numbers of every line you created or extended. A card in two lines counts twice. A line of 4 (a lot) doubles the whole turn. Playing all 4 of your cards doubles again. The game ends when the draw pile is empty and someone plays their last card — that turn counts double too. Highest score wins.

    Wild cards count 0 and can stand for any card, but must stay the same card in every line they belong to. On your turn you can swap a wild on the board for a card from your hand that fits: select the card, then tap the wild.

    House rule: if the pile is empty and everyone passes in a row, the game ends.
